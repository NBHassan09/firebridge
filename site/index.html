Method

How the two records were joined

  1. Filter. Keep detections FIRMS flags as presumed vegetation fire (type 0). Drop low confidence (MODIS < 30, VIIRS “low”). Static land sources such as brick kilns and gas flares (type 2) and offshore detections (type 3) are kept out of the counts but stay in the export.
  2. Reference. VIIRS S-NPP 375 m is the reference series from February 2012. It sees smaller, cooler fires than MODIS at 1 km.
  3. Convert MODIS. Each MODIS detection gets a weight: the S-NPP-to-MODIS ratio for its season and 0.5° cell during 2012–2021, pulled toward the season-wide ratio where data are thin. Before Aqua (to June 2002) a Terra-only ratio is used.
  4. Carry forward, live. 2026 detections come daily from NASA GIBS vector tiles of the FIRMS near-real-time feed, which needs no key. Each one is assigned to Bangladesh by comparing its distance to past Bangladeshi and neighbouring-country FIRMS detections; on held-out days this kept 99.2% of true Bangladesh detections, 98.5% of those kept were correct, and monthly counts came within 1% of the official file. Known kiln and flare sites are flagged as static sources. S-NPP stopped delivering on 2 November 2026, so from November the series uses NOAA-20, converted with the same estimator (2019–2025). Over January–October 2026 the converted NOAA-20 came to 94% of S-NPP (March 84%, April 96%), inside the 94–109% range of earlier years. NOAA-21 is linked on the 2026 overlap.
  5. Context. Each hotspot is tagged with MODIS land cover (MCD12Q1, IGBP classes) for its year. Monthly rainfall, humidity, maximum temperature, topsoil wetness and aerosol optical depth come from NASA POWER (GPM IMERG-corrected precipitation and MERRA-2), averaged over the grid points that cover the area. Aerosol over Bangladesh is dominated by regional pollution, which rose while fires fell, so it is shown as air-quality context, not as a measure of smoke.
  6. Uncertainty. Estimated months carry an 80% range from leave-one-year-out errors, which grows as counts shrink.

Data: NASA FIRMS country yearly summaries, standard processing (MCD14ML C6.1; VNP14IMG and VJ114IMG v2), November 2000 to December 2025, plus near-real-time VIIRS (S-NPP, NOAA-20, NOAA-21) for 2026 from NASA GIBS vector tiles, updated daily. NASA GIBS: MODIS MCD12Q1 land cover 2001–2024, Blue Marble, VIIRS Black Marble. NASA POWER monthly climate 2001–2025. Months from 2026 need near-real-time FIRMS data, which needs a FIRMS map key.
